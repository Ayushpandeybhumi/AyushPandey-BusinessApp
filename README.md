# OfficeRide – Corporate Employee Transport Service

Live class exercise (ITBT): a minimalist business website, published with GitHub Pages.
**Live site:** https://ayushpandeybhumi.github.io/AyushPandey-BusinessApp/

## Business and problem
OfficeRide is a Vadodara corporate commute provider. Companies struggle to give employees safe, on-time rides, and employees cannot see where their vehicle is. The site shows services and prices, tracks a ride by Trip ID, and gives managers a fleet dashboard.

## Sections (all on one page, linked from the top menu)
Home · About Business (with company profile) · Services (price table) · Offers (4 live posters + Premium membership) · Reviews · Dashboard (6 KPIs + chart) · Track a ride (interactive feature) · My account (sample customer view) · Contact / Enquiry

## Interactive feature
Track order: enter a Trip ID (OR1001 to OR1009) to see status, location, ETA, vehicle, driver and contact.

## Dashboard extras
Live trips board (trips in progress, delayed trip highlighted) and a Recent trips list on the Track page. Both open the tracker when clicked.

## Dashboard downloads
CSV (KPIs, weekly trips, live trips; opens in Excel or Numbers), Save as PDF (print view of the dashboard only) and chart image (PNG).

## Enquiry form
Sends straight to the owner's inbox through FormSubmit. The first submission from the live site asks the owner to confirm by email once.

## Plan finder
Rule-based recommender inside Services: employees, shift, location spread, priority, safety and executive trips give a ranked list with match %, estimated monthly cost and reasons. "Request this quote" pre-fills the enquiry form. Rules are listed under "How we match" on the page.

## Hyperlinks and their purpose
Home → Services (start buying), Services → Contact (request quote), About → Services, Dashboard → Track a ride, Google Maps (find office), Email and phone links (reach sales), LinkedIn (company profile), MoRTH (regulatory reference), Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya (knowledge partner).

## Two-minute presentation
1. Business: OfficeRide, corporate employee transport in Vadodara.
2. Problem: employees do not know when their ride arrives and managers lack a single view of fleet performance.
3. Customer: companies with 50 to 5,000 employees on shifts.
4. App: services and prices, live ride tracking by Trip ID, enquiry form.
5. Dashboard: trips completed, vehicles available, on-time pickup, delays, fuel consumption and satisfaction, plus a weekly chart.
