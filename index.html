Built around the people who commute.

OfficeRide is a Vadodara-based transport partner for companies. We run fixed-route shuttles, shared cabs and night-shift drops, and take care of vehicles, drivers, routing and compliance.

What we do

We design commute routes around your shifts, supply vetted drivers and well-maintained vehicles, and report performance every day.

Target customers

IT parks, manufacturing plants, BPOs, hospitals and campuses with 50 to 5,000 employees on single or multiple shifts.

Service area

Vadodara city and industrial belts: Alkapuri, Gotri, Manjalpur, Makarpura GIDC, Waghodia Road, Vasna and Karelibaug. Outstation on request.

Value proposition

94%+ on-time pickups, women-safety protocols for every night trip, and one dashboard that shows managers what matters first.

Company profile

OfficeRide was founded in 2019 by Ayush Pandey on a simple belief: a commute should not decide how an employee's day begins. We started with two shuttles serving one IT park in Gotri. Today we run a 104-vehicle fleet for 14 companies across Vadodara, with one mission: get every employee to work and back home safely and on time.

How we match

Each service starts with a base score. Points are added or removed by simple rules on group size, location spread, shift, priority and safety needs. Costs use our public price list: shuttle ₹2,800, cab pool ₹4,500 per employee a month; safe drop ₹180 a trip (22 working days, 2 trips a day assumed); executive transfer ₹1,200 a trip. Bulk contracts need 100+ employees and are quoted individually. Results are estimates, not a binding quote.
